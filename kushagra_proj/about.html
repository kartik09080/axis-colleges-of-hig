About Axis Colleges

Over a decade of academic excellence, innovation, and holistic student development in Uttar Pradesh.

Our Legacy

A Premier Hub for Technical & Professional Education

Established in 2010 in Rooma, Kanpur, Axis Colleges of Higher Education was founded with a clear mission: to bridge the gap between classroom theory and modern industrial practice.

Our programs are duly approved by AICTE, New Delhi and the Pharmacy Council of India (PCI), with affiliations to AKTU, Lucknow and CSJMU, Kanpur.

🔭 Our Vision

To emerge as a nationally respected center of higher learning recognized for ethical leadership, research culture, and high employability.

🎯 Our Mission

To deliver outcome-based education, hands-on laboratory training, and industry mentorship accessible to students from every background.
